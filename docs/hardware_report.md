## Recommendation

Use **`qwen3:8b` at `num_ctx = 8192`**. This is the IGNIS default (`IGNIS_OLLAMA_MODEL`).

- At 8k and 16k context it returned valid schema-constrained JSON in **9/9** runs. `llama3.1:8b` managed 7/9:
  it produced invalid JSON in 2 of the 6 runs at 8k and 16k.
- At the 8k context the Copilot uses, it is faster end-to-end: median 1.4 s versus 6.1 s for a 160-token
  answer to a ~3k-token prompt.
- It fits entirely in the 8 GB of VRAM. Measured GPU use was ~5.9 GB with the model loaded, leaving headroom
  for the display.

Do not load both models at the same time: together they would not fit in 8 GB of VRAM.

**Caveats.**

- "Loaded size / In VRAM" were read from `ollama ps` right after the first load, at a 2048-token context.
  Larger contexts enlarge the KV cache; the drop to ~31 tok/s for `qwen3:8b` at 16k suggests memory pressure.
  IGNIS therefore caps the context at 8k.
- The golden-set evaluation (TEST_REPORT.md) ran with `qwen3:8b`. Its median per-question latency of ~9 s
  includes retrieval, reranking and NLI validation.
