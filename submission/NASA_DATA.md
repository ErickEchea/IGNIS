# NASA and partner-agency data used by IGNIS

## NASA data and resources

- **Physical Sciences Informatics (PSI): Combustion Science investigations** (24 investigations, CC0). Search:
  https://psi.nasa.gov/physci/repo/search?q=combustion
  - FLEX (PSI-69): https://psi.nasa.gov/physci/repo/data/investigations/PSI-69, DOI 10.60555/mbq8-0451
  - BASS-II (PSI-25): https://psi.nasa.gov/physci/repo/data/investigations/PSI-25, DOI 10.60555/4qc4-de67
  - SAFFIRE-I, II and III (PSI-98, 99, 100); BASS (PSI-26); FLEX-2 (PSI-68); CFI (PSI-39); ACME (PSI-10, 20, 21,
    22, 23, 159); SAME and SAME-R (PSI-102, 101); SPICE (PSI-107); SLICE (PSI-106); DAFT (PSI-47); plus ground
    and model studies (PSI-60, 62, 115, 117, 142).

## Partner agencies

No partner-agency dataset is used in this version:

- JAXA DARTS Group Combustion data are droplet-cloud, microgravity only, and outside our partial-gravity scope.
- ESA HREDA requires a login.
- No CSA combustion dataset was found.
